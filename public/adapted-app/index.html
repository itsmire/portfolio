Great question! Here's a simple way to think about it:
GRAMMAR RULE: FOR VS SINCE

Use FOR with a duration (a period of time).
Use SINCE with a starting point (a specific time).

"I have lived here for 5 years." (duration)
"I have lived here since 2019." (starting point)
